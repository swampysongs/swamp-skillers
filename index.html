Swamp Skillers

A little OSRS-style idle game you play by typing in the live chat of my piano radio. Pick a skill, keep chatting, and your character levels up in Lumbridge while the music plays.

Getting started

  1. Open the live radio and type !join in the chat.
    Swamp Skillers @Ana joined Lumbridge! Try !train wc. Chat at least once an hour or you idle out
  2. Pick a skill. !train wc is a good first one.
    Swamp Skillers @Ana you're now chopping trees at Lumbridge woods, about 2.0 a minute. Swamp trees unlock at level 8.
  3. Chat at least once an hour. Any message counts, even "hi". After 1 hour with no chat your character idles out and stops. Type anything to jump back in.
    Swamp Skillers @Cal idled out after 1 h with no chat, so Fishing stopped. Type anything to jump back in
  4. Check in with !stats and !bank, sell with !sell all, buy better tools with !shop, and type !go when an event starts.
How fast? Each skill starts at about 2.0 actions a minute and climbs to about 4.0 by level 60 (4.2 at 99), and better tools add on top. Levels follow the real OSRS xp table, so 99 is a long road. As you level, better items unlock and show up in your catch more and more often. The bot tells you when something new unlocks:
Swamp Skillers @Ana Woodcutting level 5!
Swamp Skillers @Ana Woodcutting level 6! Steel axe in !shop.
Swamp Skillers @Ana Woodcutting 8: swamp trees can now be chopped!

Rare finds. Every item you get has a small chance of a bonus:

  • 1 in 300: a casket of coins when fishing (sometimes with a gem), a bird nest when chopping, an uncut gem when mining, a tip from the Cook when cooking, an extra bar or tool when smithing, a rare drop when fighting, a traveller's thanks in coins when burning logs, a spare log when fletching.
  • 1 in 1,000: a big bass, swordfish or shark when you catch one, a wall trophy worth real coins.
  • 1 in 20,000: a golden item (golden fish, golden nest, golden nugget, golden spatula, golden hammer, golden scimitar, golden tinderbox, golden knife). Worth 25,000 coins, but most people keep it. !sell all never sells it.

Events. Goblin raids pay coins, and shoals give you swordfish to cook and sell. See Events.

Shop and gear

Better gear means more items a minute (or more damage for swords). Your best piece in the bank counts automatically, but only once you have its level. You start with a bronze axe, a bronze pickaxe and a small net. At level 10 in Woodcutting, Fishing, Mining and Attack the Lumbridge tutor gives you the next tool for free if you don't have a better one. After that, !buy it or make it with !craft (bronze to rune). "Catch rate" is how much more you land at the level you can first use it.

Group events

About every 30 minutes, while people are chatting, something happens in Lumbridge and the bot announces it. Type !go within 2 minutes to join. The more people join, the bigger everyone's reward.

Goblin raid Attack

Swamp Skillers Goblins raid Lumbridge! !go to defend (2 min)

300 Attack xp per skiller who joins (up to 3,000), plus 50 coins + 10 per skiller

Shooting star Mining

Swamp Skillers A shooting star crashed by the swamp! !go to mine it

200 Mining xp per skiller (up to 2,000), plus 1-3 stardust

Shoal Fishing

Swamp Skillers A shoal surfaces in the River Lum! !go to fish

250 Fishing xp per skiller (up to 2,500), plus 3 raw swordfish

Cook's feast Cooking

Swamp Skillers The Cook needs food for the Duke! !go to deliver

You hand in up to 5 of your cooked fish, best first, for 2x their normal Cooking xp

Swamp Skillers Star mined by 2 skillers: +400 Mining xp each plus stardust. Next event 28 min
Swamp Skillers Feast served: 2 skillers delivered 10 fish (+600 Cooking xp). Next event 28 min
Event streak. Each event you join in a row gives +5% xp on everything you train, up to +25%. Miss 2 in a row while you're around and the streak resets. The streak bonus only counts while you're chatting (a message in the last 20 minutes).
Lamps. Shooting stars give stardust. Every 5 stardust turns into a lamp by itself. !lamp wc (or any skill) gives 1,000 xp in that skill. Careful: !sell all sells lamps and stardust, so rub them first.

Pets

Every skill has one pet, and it is extremely rare. Each time you get something (a fish, a log, an ore, a cooked fish, a bar, a burned or fletched log, a kill) there is a tiny chance a pet starts following you. The odds get a little better as you level. Getting one is the only time the game shouts: the bot says it in chat and it goes up on the stream.

You on stream

Your character walks around the 3D Lumbridge on the radio, doing what you train: chopping, fishing, mining, smelting or fighting, holding your best axe, pickaxe or sword. The event log on screen shows what players are up to, level-ups, rare finds and events. Firemaking and Fletching show on the event log; the Lumbridge avatars don't act them out yet.

FAQ

Why didn't the bot answer me?

Replies are bundled, so several answers arrive together in one message, roughly once a minute when chat is busy. Some answers show on the stream's screen instead of in chat. Also check that the message starts with !, that you waited 10 seconds since your last command, and that you didn't send the same command twice within 60 seconds. Made-up commands get no reply. !go never gets its own reply: you see it in the event result.

My character stopped. What happened?

Either you idled out (no chat for 1 hour) or you ran out of materials (no raw fish to cook, no ore to smelt, no logs to burn or fletch). Type anything to come back after an idle-out. If you ran out, the bot tells you what's missing and which command gets more.

Do I level while I'm away?

Yes, for up to 1 hour after your last message, at full speed. Event streaks only count while you're chatting (last 20 minutes). After 1 hour your character stops until you chat again.

Can I play from my phone?

Yes. It's all chat, so anywhere you can type in YouTube live chat works.

How do I delete my character?

Type !quit, then !quit yes within a minute. Everything is removed for good. See the privacy policy for what the game keeps.

Is this the real OSRS?

No. It's my own small fan game inspired by Old School RuneScape, with OSRS names for skills, places and items. It isn't made or endorsed by Jagex.
